Referral Program Terms

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Eligibility

The “50.” referral program is open to customers of Good Steward Structures who have placed an order with us. Each participant may enroll using their own contact information and may generate and share multiple unique referral links.

How rewards are earned

A participant (“Referrer”) shares a unique referral link with another individual (“Referred Customer”). If the Referred Customer submits their contact information through that link and subsequently places and completes an order for a building, both the Referrer and the Referred Customer will each receive a $50 digital gift card.

A reward is issued only after Good Steward Structures confirms the referred order has actually been placed and a qualifying deposit received. Cancelled, refunded, or unfulfilled orders do not qualify for a reward. Each referral link may be used once; a Referrer may share as many separate links as they like and will be rewarded for each one that results in a qualifying order.

Reward details

Rewards are issued as digital gift cards through our reward provider and delivered by email. Gift cards have no cash value beyond their stated denomination and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Fraud and abuse

Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, and referrals submitted without a genuine interest in purchasing are prohibited. Good Steward Structures reserves the right to withhold or reverse a reward, and to suspend a participant's access to the program, if we reasonably believe the program is being abused.

Taxes

Referral rewards may constitute taxable income depending on applicable law. Participants are responsible for determining and reporting any tax obligations arising from rewards received. Good Steward Structures does not provide tax advice.

Program changes

Good Steward Structures may modify, suspend, or terminate this program, including the reward amount, at any time. Changes will not affect rewards already earned for orders completed before the change takes effect.

Privacy

Participation involves sharing a referral link and, for the Referred Customer, providing contact and order information in the normal course of requesting a quote. This information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this program can be directed to joseph@goodstewardstructures.com.
