Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

What this covers

This policy describes how Good Steward Structures collects and uses personal information through our “50.” referral program — the pages where a customer shares a referral link and where a referred friend submits their contact information to request a quote.

Information we collect

When you use the referral program, we collect: your name; your email address and, if provided, phone number; and any message you enter describing what you're looking for. If you were referred by someone, we also record who referred you, so both of you can receive the referral reward once applicable.

How we use this information

We use this information to: contact you about your building inquiry; track and issue referral rewards; and send you the referral link or reward-related emails you'd expect from participating in the program. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

We share information with service providers who help us operate the referral program, and only for that purpose: our email delivery provider (Postmark), for sending referral links and notifications; our database and hosting providers, who store the information securely; and our gift card provider (Tremendous), if and when a reward is issued, which requires sharing the recipient's name and email or phone with them to deliver the reward.

How long we keep it

We retain referral and lead information for as long as reasonably needed to administer the program, respond to your inquiry, and maintain business records, and may retain it longer where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us using the information below, and we will do so unless we're required to retain it for a legitimate business or legal reason.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be directed to joseph@goodstewardstructures.com.
