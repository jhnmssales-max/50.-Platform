How to actually get referrals out of this

The program runs itself once a customer has their link. Everything in here is about the ninety seconds before that — the handoff — and the handful of habits that turn a quiet month into a steady one. Read it once, then keep the two scripts.

The whole thing in five steps

  1. You finish a job. A happy customer is the only raw material this needs.
  2. You add them on the dashboard — name, email, phone. That sends them their own referral link by email, over your name, immediately.
  3. They pass the link to somebody. A text, a Facebook comment, a neighbour over the fence. The link is theirs and it does not expire.
  4. That person fills in the short form on the other end of the link, and lands in your list as a new inquiry with the referrer's name attached.
  5. You close it, then mark the referral paid. Two e-gift cards go out — one to the customer who referred, one to the person who bought.
The only step that needs a human is step 2. Every month that underperforms, it is because somebody finished a job and never added the customer — not because the referral offer was not good enough.

Say it out loud at the handoff

The email does the explaining. Your job is to make sure it is opened, and that takes one sentence at the moment they are happiest — standing there looking at the finished work, not three weeks later.

At the handoff "One more thing before I go — I'm going to email you a referral link. If you send somebody our way and they end up buying, you both get an e-gift card. Takes you one text. Watch for it in the next few minutes so it doesn't get buried."

Three things are doing work in there: both get a card, it costs them one text, and it is arriving now so they should look for it. Say it in your own words, keep those three.

If they say "sure, send it"

Add them on the dashboard before you get in the truck. Not at the end of the day.

If they hesitate

No pressure version "No obligation at all — it just sits in your inbox. If somebody asks who did the work, you'll have something to send them."

The follow-up that actually matters

A customer who shared their link within the first week usually shares it again. A customer who never opened the email never will, and that is the one worth a phone call.

Open People you've invited on the dashboard. Two flags are worth acting on the day you see them:

What you seeWhat to do
Bounced The address is wrong, so nothing you send arrives. Get the right email and add them again — it is one minute and it recovers a referral you had already earned.
Unsubscribed They asked to stop being emailed. Respect it. Their link still works, so copy it and text it to them if you have that kind of relationship. Nothing further is sent.

Everyone else: the system sends its own reminder later, so you do not have to chase. If you want to, Resend on their row sends the same link again with nothing lost.

When a referral comes in

  1. Call them the same day. They were sent by somebody they trust, which makes this the warmest lead you will get all week and the easiest one to let go cold.
  2. Mention the referrer by name. "Dana said you might be looking" opens a conversation that "I'm following up on a web inquiry" does not.
  3. Quote it like any other job. The gift cards are not a discount and do not come out of your price.
  4. Mark it paid once it closes — on the Referrals list, flip the switch on that row. That is what sends both cards.
Marking paid cannot be undone, and it is what triggers the cards and any fee on your account. Flip it when the job is actually sold, not when it looks likely.

Two habits that double the numbers

1. Add everybody, not the ones you think will refer

The referrals that close come from the customers nobody expected. It costs nothing to add somebody who never shares, and the one who surprises you pays for the whole program.

2. Put it where customers already are

Your link page is yours to point at. Mention the program on the invoice, in your email signature, on the yard sign, in the Facebook post with the finished photos. The customers who already recommend you by word of mouth are the ones who will use it — they are simply not being handed anything to send.

What to say when somebody asks how it works

In one breath "You get a link. Send it to anybody who might need what we do. If they buy, you get an e-gift card and so do they. That's the whole thing."

If they want the fine print, every page has a terms link at the bottom with your own eligibility wording on it. You can change that wording — and every other sentence your customers read — from Settings → Your wording, any time, without asking anybody.
